# Delphia

> Delphia (Delphia Technologies Inc., Toronto, Canada) is an AI-driven quantitative
> investment firm that leverages alternative data to power systematic trading
> strategies. Once a consumer robo-advisor and data-reward token platform, Delphia
> pivoted in 2024 to institutional quant investing. Delphia does not currently
> publish a public developer API, SDK, or developer portal.

## Company
- [Website](https://delphia.com): Company home
- [Contact](https://delphia.com/contact): Contact and Toronto mailing address (support@delphia.com)

## Products
- Oracle Alpha: Institutional hedge fund (launched May 2024)
- Tilt: Trend-based investment platform (launched November 2024)
- Backchannel: Expert-knowledge monetization platform (announced)

  separate company, not first-party to Delphia.
